Working prediction recorded before the penalty sweep and final runs.

Increasing rho from 1 to 100 should make overload less attractive in a
tournament, so a larger share of offspring should fit the servers. The
effect may be small: CPU latency already discourages concentrated loads
and the instance has spare aggregate capacity. The best feasible costs
should be close across the three weights. A very large penalty can also
remove parents whose children would have become feasible after mutation.

Decision rule fixed before tuning: use rho=10 as the starting setting.
Choose the weight with the fewest infeasible tuning runs, then the lowest
mean feasible cost. Treat differences under 0.000001 as ties. Prefer 10
among tied weights, otherwise the smaller of them. Freeze the choice
before using seeds 0-9. Compare rho=1, 10 and 100 on seeds 100-104 with
100000 evaluations, N=30 and crossover probability 0.9.

Danial's prediction, confirmed before the final runs on seeds 0-9:
“Больше допустимых потомков, но стоимость может почти не измениться”.
In English: more feasible offspring, with little change in final cost.
