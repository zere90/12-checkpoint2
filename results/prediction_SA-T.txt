The implementation and some experimental results were already

is not claimed to be a prediction registered before experimentation.

I expect Nelder-Mead tuning of T0 and Tend to produce a modest
improvement over rule-based parameters, especially under a
limited evaluation budget.

The instance has relatively low CPU and memory utilization,
but zone restrictions reduce the feasible neighbourhood.

I expect the improvement from tuning to be small relative to
variation between random seeds. With a large evaluation budget,
both configurations may reach similar final costs.

Random search should perform worse than simulated annealing,
because it does not exploit local transfer and swap moves.
